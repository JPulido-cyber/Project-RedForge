# About page refinement — Phase 1

## Narrative purpose

The About page answers one question: why should a visitor trust the engineer behind RedForge?

The page connects Jose Pulido’s military leadership background, deliberate cybersecurity transition, and long-term offensive-security direction to the engineering work published through RedForge.

## Information hierarchy

The refined page contains:

1. Engineer profile hero
2. Professional profile and actions
3. Professional snapshot
4. Transition narrative
5. Why RedForge exists
6. Engineering philosophy
7. Current mission
8. Categorized technical focus
9. Engineering records, resume, and contact actions

## Content decisions

- Offensive cybersecurity engineering is the specialization.
- Infrastructure, identity, networking, telemetry, automation, and cloud are supporting disciplines rather than competing professional identities.
- RedForge is described as an enterprise engineering platform, not an IT-lab collection.
- Military experience is connected to accountability, preparation, validation, and reproducibility without exposing sensitive service details.
- The decorative system-status progress bar and duplicated unordered skill rails were removed from the About experience.
- Location and a photograph placeholder were omitted. Location is unnecessary for the narrative, and a placeholder would add visual weight without owner-approved content.

## Design continuity

The approved platform shell, forged panels, Orbitron and Inter typography, orange accent system, spacing rhythm, and focus behavior remain intact. Layout changes organize the narrative without changing the RedForge visual language.

## Maintenance

Narrative content and technical focus groups live in `content/about.ts`. Update that source when the professional direction or verified supporting disciplines change. Do not convert aspirational direction into an unsupported completion claim.
